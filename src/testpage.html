Lucid Touch Kiosk — Test & Setup

Plug the touchscreen in and follow the list. Each step ticks itself off.

  1. 1 · Display

    The touchscreen's display. Tapping the corners in step 3 confirms it.

  2. 2 · Permission

    macOS asks whether this app may monitor input. That is how it reads the touchscreen — click Allow.

    macOS 14+ also asks about Bluetooth while scanning USB devices. Don't Allow is correct: the panel is USB.

    1. enable Lucid Touch Kiosk — if it is not listed, add it with +

    2. a permission granted while the app is running does not reach it

    Still not working?

    The grant is tied to the exact binary. After a rebuild, remove the entry in Input Monitoring and add the app again — an entry from an older build does not apply.
